CLIENT PLATFORM / PILOT BUILD

Everest CHAI Gold — wholesale commerce

A wholesale ordering experience developed for Everest Traders Inc., combining a customer storefront with operational product and inventory tools.

The client need

Everest CHAI Gold was created as a dedicated online wholesale ordering experience for Everest Traders Inc., featuring tea, coffee, machines and snacks. The goal was to make browsing and ordering simpler while keeping day-to-day catalog updates manageable.

What I developed

  • Storefront: product browsing, search, product variants and responsive detail pages.
  • Customer experience: authentication, cart, checkout flow and account foundations.
  • Administration: product and category editing, image management, stock tools and inventory history.
  • Fulfilment setup: configurable delivery zones, pickup settings and ordering rules.

Technology and scope

Next.js App Router, React, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS and Supabase were used in the pilot implementation. Payments, production email and final business rules were still subject to implementation or client approval at the latest verified checkpoint.

Privacy: This case study is an overview of client development work. No credentials, non-public pricing, customer records or unreleased admin views are published. The visual is an illustrative concept, not a screenshot of the client's system.
