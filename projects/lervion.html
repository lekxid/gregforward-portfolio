Lervion — AI learning and productivity

An evolving AI-assisted learning environment focused on notes, study workflows, planning, and more natural interactions with software.

The product

Lervion is my independent AI-powered learning and productivity product. The work explores how an AI assistant can coexist with familiar tools for organizing information, studying and managing work.

What I have worked on

  • LAI: an interactive AI learning assistant and workstation foundation.
  • JAVI: voice-assistant and hands-free interaction experiments.
  • Learning tools: notes, flashcards, quizzes and lesson-related workflows.
  • Product experience: responsive navigation, dashboards and workspace organization.

Engineering perspective

The project includes frontend/backend integration, state and continuity work, media workflows, and iterative usability refinement. Some capabilities remain in testing; this page is not a claim of a publicly available finished product.

Development status: Active, evolving product. A public demo or repository is not linked until it is verified and safe to publish.
